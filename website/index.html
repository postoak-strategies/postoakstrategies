What does this cost?

We quote a fixed price after the free call, scoped to what you actually need. No hourly surprises.

My books are a mess. Can you help?

That's a big part of the job. We find the errors and show you exactly what to fix. The corrections are made by your bookkeeper or accountant; we don't do tax or audit work.

Do I have to switch software?

No. We work with what you have, like QuickBooks, spreadsheets or your job-management tool, usually starting from an export.

Our books are in good shape. Can you help with something else?

Absolutely. Plenty of our work has nothing to do with accounting: automating quotes and follow-ups, intake forms, scheduling, reports, internal tools, and connecting the apps you already use. If it eats up your team's time, we can probably automate it.

Is my data safe?

We ask for the least access that gets the job done, agree on terms before any data changes hands, and never ask for passwords by email. Details on our privacy page.
