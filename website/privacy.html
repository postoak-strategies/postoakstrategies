Privacy & data handling

How we handle your information

Last updated November 9, 2026

Short version: we collect only what you choose to send us, we use it to talk with you, and we don’t sell it. When we work together, we treat your business data with the care we’d want for our own.

Who we are

Post Oak Strategies is a small consulting business based in Houston, Texas. You can reach us at ben@postoakstrategies.com with any question about this page.

What we collect on this website

  • What you send through the contact form: your name, work email, company, and anything optional you add, such as a phone number, the tools you use, or what you’d like help with.
  • Nothing else, on purpose. This site does not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. If that ever changes, we’ll update this page first.
  • Fonts from Google. The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers when a page loads, as it does on much of the web.
  • The sample dashboard runs entirely in your browser using made-up data. Nothing you click there is sent to us.

How we use it

We use what you send to reply to you, schedule a conversation, and follow up about what you asked for. We don’t sell your information and we don’t share it for others’ marketing. Your message is handled by our email provider and, if you use the contact form, by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), the service that delivers form messages to our inbox.

How long we keep it

We keep your message only as long as it’s useful for responding to your request and following up. You can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing us, and we will.

Emails from us

If we email you, it will come from a real person at Post Oak Strategies and say who we are and why we’re writing. Every message that isn’t a direct reply includes a way to opt out, and we honor opt-outs right away.

How we handle your business data if we work together

Some projects involve financial or operational data, such as an export from your accounting system. Our approach:

  • Agreement first. We agree on scope, confidentiality, and how data will be shared before any business data changes hands.
  • Least access. We ask for the smallest amount of access that gets the job done, read-only wherever possible, usually a dated export rather than a live login.
  • No passwords by email or chat. We’ll never ask you to send passwords or account credentials that way.
  • Your data stays yours. We use it only for your project. We don’t use it for other clients or to train AI models.
  • AI tools only with your say-so. We don’t put your business data into an AI service unless you’ve authorized it, and we’ll tell you which one.
  • Numbers are checked. Totals come from your data and are reconciled against your own reports. AI may help explain results, but it doesn’t make up figures.
  • Clean exit. When the work ends, we’ll delete your data on request and confirm it.

Every project is different, and the specifics go in writing before we start.

Your choices

You can ask what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Just email ben@postoakstrategies.com.
